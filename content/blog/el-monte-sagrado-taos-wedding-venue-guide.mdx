If Taos weddings are usually intimate and rustic by nature, El Monte Sagrado is the exception — a full-service luxury resort inside Taos town limits, built around waterfalls, aspen groves, and a genuine sacred circle ceremony space, for couples who want resort polish without leaving the mountains.

## What makes El Monte Sagrado special

The resort's name means "sacred mountain," and the property is designed around that idea: aspen trees, water features, and a dedicated Sacred Circle space built specifically for ceremonies, alongside more conventional indoor event spaces for larger receptions. It's a rare combination in Taos — a property with true resort infrastructure (spa, multiple dining venues, full-service event planning) set inside a town better known for adobe casitas and gorge-rim elopements.

Because El Monte Sagrado offers everything from an intimate 8-guest Wine Room to a 400-guest ballroom, it's one of the only Taos venues that can flex across that entire range without switching properties.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Rio Grande Ballroom** — the resort's largest indoor space, with reported capacities ranging up to 400 guests depending on configuration; plan to confirm your exact layout and seating plan directly with the venue.
- **Sacred Circle** — an intimate outdoor ceremony space built for smaller gatherings, from elopements around 20 guests up to celebrations in the low hundreds.
- **The Wine Room** — an intimate indoor space holding up to 8 guests, suited to a small rehearsal dinner or a truly private elopement dinner.
- **Library and Gallery spaces** overlooking the resort's aspen trees and waterfalls, offering a mid-sized indoor option between the Wine Room and the full ballroom.
- **Full-service resort infrastructure**: on-site spa, multiple dining venues, and an events team accustomed to executing both large receptions and intimate elopements on the same property.

## Best photo spots

- The **Sacred Circle**, the resort's purpose-built ceremony space and its most requested backdrop.
- The **aspen groves and waterfalls** framing the Library and Gallery spaces, especially striking in fall color.
- The **Rio Grande Ballroom's interior**, for couples who want a polished, resort-scale reception backdrop.
- The **resort's water features** throughout the property, used extensively for both ceremony backdrops and portrait sessions.

## Planning a wedding at El Monte Sagrado

A full-service resort inside Taos changes the planning calculus — instead of coordinating a patchwork of small, independent vendors the way many Taos weddings require, you're working with an established events team who already knows the property's spaces, capacities, and catering capabilities. Our job shifts to making sure your specific vision, your outside vendors where you want them, and the resort's own team are all working from the same plan.

We also help couples choose the right space for their guest count here specifically because the range is so wide — the Wine Room, Sacred Circle, Library, Gallery, and full ballroom all serve genuinely different kinds of weddings, and picking the wrong one is an easy mistake to make without local guidance.
