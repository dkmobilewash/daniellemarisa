The newest addition to Santa Fe's wedding scene comes from an unexpected source: the team behind Coyote Café and Santacafe, two of the city's most established restaurants, who built The Sunset Terrace as a dedicated event space rather than another dining room. The result is a venue where the food and bar program is the headline, not an afterthought.

## What makes The Sunset Terrace special

Set in the heart of downtown Santa Fe, The Sunset Terrace centers on an expansive balcony wrapped in French doors, overlooking the city and the Sangre de Cristo Mountains beyond. Indoor lounges, a full bar, and a dining room with a stage that doubles as an open kitchen give the space a chic, restaurant-forward energy rather than a traditional banquet-hall feel.

Because the venue is fully remote-controlled — lighting, both indoor and outdoor, runs on color-changing LEDs — couples have a level of atmosphere control that's unusual for a downtown Santa Fe property, without needing to bring in a separate lighting designer.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Up to 300 guests**, with flexible configuration across the indoor lounges, dining room, and outdoor terrace.
- **In-house catering** from the team behind Coyote Café and Santacafe — two of Santa Fe's most decorated restaurant kitchens — meaning your menu and bar program come with real culinary pedigree built in.
- **Full-service rental**: the venue provides catering, bartending, cleanup, and rental basics like altar, tables, chairs, flatware, linens, and glassware, simplifying the vendor list considerably.
- **Outdoor terrace with fire pits and lounge seating**, giving guests a natural flow between dancing, dining, and quieter conversation areas.
- **Downtown location**: as with any downtown Santa Fe venue, load-in timing and street access during peak season are worth confirming early.

## Best photo spots

- The **balcony overlooking downtown Santa Fe**, with the Sangre de Cristo Mountains as the backdrop — the venue's signature view.
- The **French doors and indoor lounges**, for a warmer, restaurant-chic set of reception photos.
- The **fire pit lounge areas** on the terrace, especially after dark with the LED lighting engaged.
- The **open-kitchen stage**, for couples who want their catering team and bar program woven visually into the reception itself.

## Planning a wedding at The Sunset Terrace

Because The Sunset Terrace bundles catering, bar, and most rental basics in-house, a lot of the usual vendor-coordination workload shifts into fewer conversations — which is a real advantage, but it still requires someone managing timeline, florals, photography, and the overall day-of flow so the built-in pieces and your outside vendors move together smoothly.
