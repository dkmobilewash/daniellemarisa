Recognizable from blocks away by its stepped, pueblo-inspired silhouette, the Inn and Spa at Loretto is one of downtown Santa Fe's most photographed buildings — and, conveniently, one of its most flexible wedding venues, with four distinct spaces sized for everything from a 30-guest gathering to a 280-guest celebration.

## What makes the Inn and Spa at Loretto special

The building's tiered, Taos Pueblo-inspired architecture was designed to echo the multi-story adobe structures found throughout northern New Mexico, and it remains one of the most distinct silhouettes in the city's skyline. Just steps from the Loretto Chapel and the Plaza, the hotel puts couples in Santa Fe's most walkable historic core without sacrificing full-service hotel amenities — a spa, on-site dining, and a dedicated events team.

Because the hotel offers four ballrooms of meaningfully different scale, it's a genuinely flexible venue rather than a one-size property — a rare thing this close to the Plaza.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Zuni Ballroom** — the largest space, holding up to 200 guests.
- **Tesuque Ballroom** — a mid-sized option for up to 150 guests.
- **Outdoor garden**, recently renovated, accommodating up to 150 guests for an open-air ceremony or reception.
- **The Penthouse** — an intimate space for gatherings up to 30, ideal for a rehearsal dinner or an elopement-scale wedding.
- **Overall property maximum** sits around 280 guests, giving couples real room to choose the right-sized space rather than over- or under-filling one fixed ballroom.
- **Walkable location**: the hotel is immediately adjacent to the Loretto Chapel and a short walk to the Plaza, simplifying ceremony-to-reception logistics if you're using more than one location downtown.

## Best photo spots

- The **hotel's tiered pueblo-style exterior**, one of the most recognizable architectural backdrops in Santa Fe.
- The **Loretto Chapel next door**, famous for its "miraculous staircase," often used by guests staying at the inn for ceremony photos even when the reception is elsewhere.
- The **renovated outdoor garden**, for a softer, greenery-forward set of portraits downtown.
- The **Penthouse's views**, for an intimate rooftop-adjacent backdrop over the city.

## Planning a wedding at the Inn and Spa at Loretto

With four ballrooms of different sizes on one property, the first real planning decision here is matching your guest count and vision to the right room — a 60-guest wedding in the Zuni Ballroom will feel cavernous, while the same guest count in the Penthouse or garden will feel exactly right. We've planned events across the property's spaces and help couples make that call early, before a deposit locks you into the wrong room.

Because the hotel sits directly next to the Loretto Chapel and a short walk from the Plaza, we also handle the choreography of a multi-location Santa Fe wedding day — timing a chapel ceremony, guest transition, and hotel reception so nothing feels rushed.
