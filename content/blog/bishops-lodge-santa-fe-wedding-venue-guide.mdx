Tucked into the foothills a few minutes north of downtown, Bishop's Lodge is Santa Fe's answer for couples who want a true luxury-resort wedding weekend without leaving the city's orbit. As part of the Auberge Resorts Collection, it brings a level of polish — and a price point — to match.

## What makes Bishop's Lodge special

The property sits on land with genuine history, framed by piñon-juniper foothills and the Little Tesuque Creek, and the resort's design leans into that setting rather than competing with it: low adobe-inspired architecture, expansive outdoor terraces, and a small chapel on the grounds that couples use for intimate ceremonies. It's quieter and more secluded than a downtown hotel, which is exactly the point for couples planning a multi-day wedding weekend for out-of-town guests.

Because it's an Auberge property, the level of service extends well past the wedding day itself — spa treatments, a full activities program, and a culinary team used to executing high-end, personalized menus for weddings rather than banquet-standard plated dinners.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Eight distinct event spaces** on property, with occupancies ranging from intimate gatherings up to the low hundreds depending on configuration.
- **Right-sized for intimacy**: most wedding planners recommend keeping guest counts around 150, with 175 as a comfortable maximum, for the best experience across the resort's event spaces.
- **9,700 square feet** of versatile event space overall, including a 3,200-square-foot ballroom for indoor receptions.
- **Garden ceremony space** outside the resort's chapel, holding up to 200 at maximum with 150 recommended as the ideal count for comfort.
- **Multi-day weekend logistics**: because Bishop's Lodge functions as a full resort, welcome dinners, spa mornings, and next-day brunches can all happen on property without guests needing to leave.

## Best photo spots

- The **chapel and its surrounding gardens**, the resort's signature ceremony backdrop.
- The **foothills and piñon-juniper landscape** framing the property, especially at golden hour.
- The **outdoor terraces**, used for cocktail hours with views back toward the Sangre de Cristo range.
- The **creek-side grounds**, a quieter, greener counterpoint to Santa Fe's high-desert palette elsewhere in the city.

## Planning a wedding at Bishop's Lodge

A resort at this level runs on its own well-oiled event process, and the planning work is less about wrangling logistics and more about making sure your vision, your outside vendors (if any), and the resort's in-house team are all rowing in the same direction from day one.
