There has been an inn on this exact corner of the Santa Fe Plaza since the city's earliest days as a trading post, and the current La Fonda — in continuous operation since the 1920s — remains the most historically significant wedding venue in the city. For couples who want their wedding literally in the center of Santa Fe, there isn't a more iconic address.

## What makes La Fonda special

Every surface at La Fonda tells you where you are: hand-painted glass, carved corbels, wrought iron, and tin work by generations of local artisans fill the hotel's public spaces. The ballrooms carry that same hand-crafted detail up into 25-foot ceilings hung with pounded tin chandeliers — a look that can't be replicated by a newer property, because it was built by the same families who still do this work in Santa Fe today.

Because La Fonda sits directly on the Plaza, a La Fonda wedding also means your guests can walk to the Cathedral Basilica, the Palace of the Governors, and Canyon Road galleries between events — the whole historic district becomes an extension of your venue.

## Capacity & logistics

- **20,112 square feet** of event space across eight distinct venues, giving La Fonda more configuration flexibility than almost any other Santa Fe property.
- **Up to 600 guests** across the full property; the primary ballroom alone comfortably holds up to 300 for a seated reception.
- **25-foot ceilings and pounded tin chandeliers** in the main ballroom, a signature look that anchors most La Fonda wedding photography.
- **Plaza-adjacent logistics**: because the hotel sits in Santa Fe's busiest historic district, load-in timing, street closures during peak tourist season, and noise ordinances downtown all need real local knowledge to plan around.
- **On-site lodging**: as a full-service historic hotel, La Fonda can host your wedding weekend's room block on property, which simplifies guest logistics considerably for destination weddings.

## Best photo spots

- The **main ballroom's tin chandeliers and hand-painted ceiling beams**, the single most photographed detail at the property.
- **La Terraza rooftop**, when available, for skyline views over the Plaza and the Sangre de Cristo Mountains beyond.
- The **hotel's hand-painted windows and stairwells**, which read as unmistakably Santa Fe and unmistakably La Fonda.
- The **Plaza itself**, a two-minute walk from the lobby, for couples who want the obelisk, the portal, and Santa Fe's historic core in their gallery.
