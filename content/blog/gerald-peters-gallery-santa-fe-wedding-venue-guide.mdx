For couples who want their wedding to feel like an art opening rather than a banquet, Gerald Peters Gallery is Santa Fe's most distinctive alternative to a hotel ballroom — a working contemporary and Western art gallery with a sculpture garden built for exactly this kind of evening.

## What makes Gerald Peters Gallery special

Located on Paseo de Peralta near the heart of Santa Fe's historic district, the gallery offers two very different aesthetics under one roof: traditional Western art galleries with warm, gallery-quality lighting, and stark white contemporary spaces for a more minimal, editorial feel. Couples can choose which mood fits their day, or use both.

The real signature of the property is its sculpture garden — a lush, sky-high outdoor space anchored by a koi pond, ringed by rotating sculpture installations that mean no two weddings here look quite the same. It's one of the few Santa Fe venues where the art itself is part of the decor, not something rented in for the day.

## Capacity & logistics

- **8,500 total square feet** of event space, with a standing capacity of 200 and a seated capacity of 180 — a strong fit for mid-sized weddings that want something more distinctive than a standard ballroom.
- **Sculpture garden with koi pond**, used for ceremonies, cocktail hours, or as an open-air complement to an indoor reception.
- **Rental structure**: gallery rental typically covers a 6-hour event window, with setup and cleanup time booked separately — worth building explicitly into your day-of timeline.
- **Outside catering allowed**, and alcohol service is permitted, giving couples more vendor flexibility than a venue with an exclusive in-house caterer.
- **Working gallery**: because it's an active commercial gallery, install schedules and current exhibitions can affect which spaces are available on a given date — this is worth confirming early in planning.

## Best photo spots

- The **sculpture garden and koi pond**, especially with whatever large-scale installation is on view that season.
- The **contemporary white gallery spaces**, for clean, editorial portraits with gallery lighting doing the work.
- The **traditional Western galleries**, for a warmer, more classic portrait backdrop among framed paintings.
- The **outdoor patio transitions between galleries**, where Santa Fe's blue sky and desert light frame the architecture.

## Planning a wedding at Gerald Peters Gallery

Because this is a working gallery rather than a dedicated event venue, planning here means coordinating around current exhibitions, a rental window measured in hours rather than a full-day buyout, and load-in logistics that respect the art on the walls. We've planned events in gallery spaces before and know how to sequence vendor arrivals, florals, and rental furniture so nothing risks the collection while still giving your day room to breathe.

We also help couples decide how to use the sculpture garden alongside the indoor galleries — many couples do a garden ceremony, a gallery cocktail hour, and a second gallery for the seated reception, which takes real routing and timeline planning to pull off smoothly in a single 6-hour window.
