Perched atop Outlaw Hill at 7,200 feet, with a history running back to Billy the Kid and a more recent chapter as Dennis Hopper's old hideaway, The Stakeout is Taos's most legendary wedding venue — and the one with, without much competition, the single best view in town.

## What makes The Stakeout special

The Stakeout has been a Taos landmark for decades, and its outdoor patio is the reason: a two-tier wooden deck and bar with a genuinely panoramic sweep of the Taos Valley, the Rio Grande Gorge, and the Sangre de Cristo Mountains all in one frame. Adobe arches, chimeneas, and sage and piñon scrub surround the property, giving it a rustic, high-desert-hideaway feel that matches its outlaw history.

Because the venue markets itself as New Mexico's premier all-inclusive wedding destination, much of the usual vendor coordination — catering, bar, event staff — comes bundled with the space itself, which simplifies planning considerably for couples who want the view without managing ten separate vendor contracts.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Standard seating for up to 50 guests**, making The Stakeout a strong fit for intimate weddings and elopements rather than large receptions.
- **8-hour rental period**, including setup and cleanup, with on-site staff support and a dedicated venue manager included in the package.
- **All-inclusive structure**: food, cocktails, and event staffing are built into the venue's packages, reducing the number of outside vendors a couple needs to source and manage independently.
- **Elevation and access**: at 7,200 feet atop Outlaw Hill, weather and road conditions are worth planning around, particularly for late-fall or winter dates.
- **Complimentary parking** is included, a genuine convenience for a hilltop property outside the flow of downtown Taos.

## Best photo spots

- The **two-tier wooden deck**, the venue's signature space and the source of nearly every photo the property is known for.
- The **panoramic view of the Rio Grande Gorge and Sangre de Cristo Mountains**, especially at golden hour and sunset.
- The **adobe arches and chimeneas** scattered throughout the grounds, for warm, textural detail shots.
- The **desert scrub and piñon landscape** surrounding the property, which reads as authentically high-desert Taos rather than a manicured event lawn.

## Planning a wedding at The Stakeout

An all-inclusive venue simplifies vendor sourcing, but it doesn't remove the need for someone managing your specific timeline, your family dynamics, your florals and design, and the moment-to-moment flow of the day — that's still the planner's job even when catering and bar are bundled in. We've worked with all-inclusive venues before and know how to layer in the personal details (design, florals, timeline, family logistics) that a bundled package doesn't cover on its own.

Because the property sits at elevation on a hilltop outside town, we also build a clear weather and access contingency plan into every Stakeout wedding timeline — the view is worth it, but it comes with real logistics.
