Halfway between the town of Taos and Taos Ski Valley sits Arroyo Seco, a tiny mountain village, and inside it, Saltos — a 150-year-old adobe building turned private event space that's become one of the most requested small-wedding venues in the area for couples who want rustic and elegant in the same room.

## What makes Saltos special

The building itself is the draw: a genuine 150-year-old adobe structure in Arroyo Seco, preserved rather than renovated into something generic, with the thick walls, deep window openings, and worn-in character that only come from a building that's actually been there that long. It sits in a village that's become a small food-and-culture destination in its own right, halfway up the mountain toward the ski valley.

Because Saltos books as a full private buyout rather than one of several simultaneous events, every wedding here gets the entire building and the team's full attention — there's no shared lobby, no other event down the hall.

## Capacity & logistics

- **Full buyout only**: Saltos is booked exclusively for private events, meaning your wedding has the entire historic building rather than a rented room within a larger operation.
- **Locally sourced, chef-driven menus**: the culinary team works directly with couples to build a personalized menu rather than choosing from a fixed banquet package — a real point of difference for food-focused couples.
- **Arroyo Seco setting**: located between Taos and Taos Ski Valley, the village itself adds a boutique, mountain-town feel that's distinct from both downtown Taos and the ski valley proper.
- **Scale**: because the venue is an intimate historic building rather than a purpose-built event hall, it suits smaller weddings and rehearsal dinners better than large-scale receptions — confirm current capacity directly with the venue when you inquire.

## Best photo spots

- The **historic adobe facade and courtyard**, the defining backdrop of the property.
- **Arroyo Seco's village streets**, a short walk from the venue, for a mountain-village portrait session distinct from Taos proper.
- The **interior's original adobe details** — deep window wells, viga ceilings, worn wood floors — for warm, textural reception photography.
- The **mountain light** on the drive up toward Taos Ski Valley, especially in early evening.

## Planning a wedding at Saltos

A historic full-buyout venue like this rewards a planner who treats the building with the same care the owners do — coordinating vendor load-in through a small adobe structure, working within a locally sourced menu program rather than a standard banquet kitchen, and making sure a rustic, intimate space doesn't get overwhelmed by rental furniture it was never designed to hold.

Because Arroyo Seco has a much smaller vendor pool than Taos or Santa Fe, timing and relationships matter even more here — we know which photographers, florists, and rental companies are used to working in a village this size, and how to build a day that feels effortless in a setting that's genuinely small-town.
